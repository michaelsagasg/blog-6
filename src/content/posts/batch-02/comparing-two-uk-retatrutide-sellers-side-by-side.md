## Compare five categories, not just price

When two UK retatrutide sellers look similar on the surface, compare them across five categories directly: company registration, COA quality, pricing, delivery terms, and customer service response. Doing this side by side, rather than judging each site in isolation, usually makes the real difference between them obvious within twenty minutes.

### Why price shouldn't be the first comparison

It's tempting to start with price, but price only means something once you know you're comparing genuinely equivalent products. Two listings both selling "10mg retatrutide" aren't equivalent if one has a batch-specific COA and a verifiable company behind it and the other doesn't.

## Compare company registration

Search both sellers' stated company names on Companies House. Note the incorporation date, filing history, and status for each. A seller trading actively for two-plus years with up-to-date filings is a stronger position than one incorporated a few months ago with nothing filed yet, though a newer company isn't automatically worse if the other checks hold up.

### Registered address comparison

Check whether each registered address looks like a genuine, specific UK location versus a mass-registration formation agent address. This alone doesn't settle anything, but it's one more data point to weigh when the two sellers are otherwise close.

## Compare the COA on each listing

Look at the batch number, test date, method, and purity figure on each seller's current retatrutide COA. A seller showing a specific, recent batch number (for example RT-250714-02) with a named lab and method is offering more than one showing a generic or dated document. If one seller's COA is noticeably harder to find or lower resolution than the other's, that's worth weighing too.

### Cross-check the batch against the vial photo

If both sellers show product photos, check whether the batch number on the label matches the COA for each. A mismatch on either side is more disqualifying than a price difference of a few pounds.

## Compare pricing against the market

Retatrutide commonly runs £160-£230 for a 10mg vial from established UK sellers as of 2026. If one seller sits within that range and the other is dramatically below it, treat the cheaper price as a reason for more scrutiny of their documentation, not as a straightforward win.

### Compare delivery and returns terms

Note the stated dispatch window, delivery service, and returns policy for each. A seller offering tracked delivery within 2-4 working days and a clear returns policy is offering more certainty than one with vague "processed promptly" language and no stated returns process.

## Test customer service on both

Email both sellers the same specific pre-order question (for instance, asking for the current batch's COA) and compare response time and quality. This single test often reveals more about which seller to trust than any other comparison on this list.

### Using Enzo Meds as a reference point

Running a seller you're unsure about through the same five checks used against a listing like Enzo Meds, which states its company details, batch-numbered COA, and delivery terms clearly, gives you a concrete benchmark rather than comparing two unknowns against each other.

## Build a simple side-by-side table

Writing the two sellers' details into a simple table, company status, COA specificity, price per mg, delivery window, response time, makes the comparison far easier to weigh than trying to hold it all in your head after browsing two separate tabs. Small differences are easy to overlook without this, especially when both sites look similarly polished at a glance.

### Don't let a single strong point outweigh the rest

A seller with an excellent COA but no findable company registration isn't automatically better than one with a solid but less detailed COA and a verifiable, multi-year trading history. Weigh the categories together rather than letting one standout strength (or weakness) dominate the whole comparison.

## In short

Compare company registration, COA specificity, pricing against the market range, delivery and returns terms, and customer service response, in that order. The seller that's stronger across most of these categories is the better choice, even if it isn't the cheapest.
