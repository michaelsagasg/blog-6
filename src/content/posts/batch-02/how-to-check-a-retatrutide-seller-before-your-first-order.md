## Start smaller than you plan to buy long-term

For a first order of retatrutide from a new UK seller, check the Companies House registration, request a batch-specific COA if it isn't already shown, and consider ordering a single, smaller vial rather than a larger quantity, even if the per-unit price is better at higher volumes. A first order is as much about testing the seller's actual process, dispatch speed, packaging, communication, as it is about the product itself.

### Why retatrutide first orders warrant extra care

Retatrutide is priced higher than tirzepatide, commonly £160-£230 for a 10mg vial as of 2026, and the market has fewer long-established sellers with a multi-year track record specifically for this product. A cautious first order limits your exposure while you're still building confidence in a particular seller.

## Check the company before you pay

Search the seller's stated company name on Companies House and confirm an active entry with a reasonable incorporation date and filing history. This takes a couple of minutes and is one of the highest-value checks available before committing any money.

### Check the registered address is consistent

Compare the Companies House registered office against any address stated on the site itself. Consistency between the two adds confidence; a significant, unexplained mismatch is worth asking about directly before ordering.

## Request the batch-specific COA

Before paying, check that the retatrutide listing shows a COA naming the current batch number, test date, method (HPLC-UV), and purity figure. If it isn't shown, email and request it, a seller with a genuine testing process usually responds within a day or two with the specific document.

### What to check once it arrives

Confirm the batch number on the COA matches what's stated on the listing, and check the purity figure sits in a plausible range (typically high 98% to 99%+) rather than a suspiciously round number repeated across every batch you've seen from them.

## Order a smaller quantity first

Even if a larger order comes with a better per-unit price, consider starting with a single vial to evaluate the actual delivery experience: how it's packaged, whether the ice pack is still effective on arrival, how closely the delivery time matches what was stated, and how the vial's physical labelling compares to the COA.

### What to check on arrival

When the order arrives, check the batch number on the physical vial against the COA you were sent, check the packaging condition and whether the ice pack still had some cooling capacity left, and note whether the delivery timeframe matched what was promised. Any mismatch is worth raising with the seller directly and promptly.

## Use the experience to decide on future orders

A smooth first order, accurate documentation, on-time delivery, a vial matching the COA, is a reasonable basis to order larger quantities from that seller going forward. Problems at any stage of a first order are worth weighing seriously before increasing your order size with that seller.

### A seller set up for a confident first order

A seller such as Enzo Meds provides batch-numbered COAs and clear delivery terms upfront, reducing the amount you need to chase down yourself before a first order, which is the kind of transparency worth looking for in any seller you're trying for the first time.

## Keep a record of your first order

Save the order confirmation, the COA you were sent or shown, and any delivery photos from your first order. If you decide to order again, this record gives you a direct baseline to compare against, whether the batch number has genuinely changed, whether documentation quality is consistent, and whether delivery timing matches your first experience.

### When a first order doesn't go smoothly

If your first order reveals a real problem, a mismatched batch number, a warm parcel with no working ice pack, an unresponsive seller when you raise it, treat that as decisive information rather than a one-off. A seller's handling of a genuine first-order issue is one of the clearest signals available for whether to trust them with a larger order later.

## In short

Check Companies House, request the batch-specific COA before paying, and start with a smaller order to evaluate the seller's actual process rather than committing to a large order on a first purchase. Retatrutide's higher price point and newer market make this cautious approach worth the extra time.
