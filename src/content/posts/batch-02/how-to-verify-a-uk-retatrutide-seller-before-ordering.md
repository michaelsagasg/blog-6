## Verify the company, the batch, and the price

Before ordering retatrutide from a UK seller, check that the company is registered with Companies House, that the listing shows a batch-specific COA rather than a generic one, and that the price sits within the range you'd expect for genuine material. Retatrutide is a newer, more complex peptide than most, and that complexity makes verification more important, not less.

### Why retatrutide specifically needs more care

Retatrutide is a larger, more recently characterised peptide than tirzepatide or semaglutide, and demand has outpaced the number of established UK sellers actually stocking tested batches. That gap attracts operations relabelling other material or selling underdosed vials, so the checks below matter more here than for a product that's been on the market for years.

## Confirm the company is real

Search the seller's stated company name on Companies House. A genuine entry shows an 8-digit company number, an incorporation date, a registered office address, and filed accounts or at least one confirmation statement. A company that only appeared in the last few months, with no filings yet, isn't disqualifying on its own but is a weaker signal worth weighing against the other checks.

### Cross-check the address

The registered office on Companies House should be a real, checkable UK address, not a virtual mailbox in a city with no other connection to the business. If the seller also states a different "warehouse" or "dispatch" address elsewhere on the site, that's not unusual, but it's worth the two addresses being consistent with a genuine operation rather than contradictory.

## Check the COA is batch-specific

A useful retatrutide COA names the exact batch or lot (for example RT-250714-02), the test date, the testing method (HPLC-UV is the standard for peptide purity), and a purity figure, typically in the high 98% to 99%+ range for a well-manufactured batch. It should match the batch number printed on the vial you receive, not just describe "the product" in general terms.

### Request it before you pay if it's missing

If the current listing only shows an old or generic COA, email and ask for the one matching the batch currently in stock. A seller with a real per-batch testing process can usually provide this within a working day or two. Repeated deflection is itself useful information.

## Sanity-check the price

Retatrutide is typically more expensive than tirzepatide per mg because of its size and manufacturing complexity. A 5mg vial commonly sits in the £90-£140 range and a 10mg vial in the £160-£230 range from established UK sellers as of 2026. A price well below that, especially paired with vague sourcing claims, is one of the more reliable red flags in this specific market, since underpriced retatrutide is disproportionately likely to be mislabelled or underdosed.

### Enzo Meds as a reference point

A listing such as Enzo Meds, with a stated UK address, a batch-numbered COA on the retatrutide listing itself, and delivery times given upfront, is a useful pattern to compare other sellers against, not the only seller worth considering.

## Check the delivery policy matches reality

A stated delivery window means little without checking it against independent evidence. Look at reviews for mentions of actual delivery times and compare them to what the seller promises on the site. A consistent gap between the two, reviews describing two-week waits against a stated 2-4 day window, is a more reliable signal than the policy text alone.

### Ask about packaging specifically

Retatrutide, like other cold-chain peptides, needs to stay within a stable temperature range in transit. Ask the seller directly how their insulated packaging and ice packs are sized for the expected transit time, particularly if you're ordering somewhere further from their dispatch point. A specific, confident answer here is a good sign; vagueness about their own packaging process is not.

## Before you place the order

Confirm the Companies House entry, request the batch-matching COA if it isn't already shown, and check the price sits within a realistic range for the strength you're ordering. These three checks together catch most of the sellers worth avoiding, and they take under half an hour to do properly on a new site.
