## Check three things together

To confirm a tirzepatide seller is genuinely UK-based, check Companies House for a UK registered office, the delivery policy for stated UK dispatch and realistic delivery times, and the returns address for a UK location. Any single check can be misleading on its own, since branding and domain extension are easy to set up regardless of where a business actually operates; all three checks agreeing is a much stronger confirmation.

### Why this matters for tirzepatide specifically

Tirzepatide bought from a genuinely overseas seller faces UK customs risk on import, since parcels can be delayed, inspected, or seized depending on declaration and contents. High search demand for tirzepatide means a lot of sites present themselves as UK-based in branding while actually fulfilling from elsewhere, so this check is worth doing even on sites that look convincingly British.

## Check Companies House for a UK entity

Search the seller's stated company name on Companies House. A UK limited company is legally required to have a UK registered office address. If the stated company doesn't appear on the register at all, that's a strong sign the business isn't a UK-registered entity, regardless of the site's design or spelling conventions.

### UK registration doesn't guarantee UK dispatch

A company can be UK-registered while still warehousing and shipping stock from overseas. Companies House confirms the legal entity's location, not necessarily where your parcel actually comes from, which is why checking the delivery policy separately matters.

## Check the delivery policy for UK dispatch specifics

Look for a delivery policy naming Royal Mail Tracked services or a specific UK courier, with realistic delivery windows of roughly 2-4 working days. Delivery estimates of one to three weeks, or any mention of customs clearance for a supposedly domestic order, is a strong sign the parcel is coming from overseas.

### Currency and checkout as a secondary clue

Pricing shown only in a non-GBP currency, or a checkout that converts currency at payment, is a secondary indicator worth noting, even on a site that otherwise reads as British in tone and spelling throughout.

## Check the returns address

If a returns policy is published, check whether the stated returns address is within the UK. A UK-registered company giving an overseas returns address is an inconsistency worth raising directly with the seller before you order, since it suggests the fulfilment operation may not match the registered business location.

### When to just ask

If these checks leave you unsure, email and ask directly where orders dispatch from. A genuinely UK-based seller can answer immediately and specifically; a vague or avoidant answer is informative on its own.

## What a consistent UK setup looks like

A seller such as Enzo Meds shows agreement across all three checks: a UK Companies House entry, UK dispatch with Royal Mail Tracked delivery, and UK-based contact details, which is the pattern worth expecting from any seller claiming UK operation.

## Check spelling and language conventions

A genuinely UK-based seller typically uses British spelling consistently across the whole site, including less-visited pages like the terms and delivery policy, not just the homepage. Slips into American spelling or phrasing on pages that get less editorial attention can be a sign the business, or at least its content, doesn't originate in the UK.

### Check any listed phone number

If a phone number is given, check it has a genuine UK area code consistent with a UK landline or mobile number. A stated UK address alongside a phone number that clearly isn't a UK number is a specific, checkable inconsistency worth raising directly with the seller.

## In short

Check Companies House for a genuine UK registered office, the delivery policy for UK dispatch and realistic delivery windows, and the returns address for UK consistency. Agreement across all three is a reliable confirmation that a seller is actually UK-based, not just UK-branded.
