## Check three things together

To confirm whether a retatrutide seller is genuinely UK-based, check the Companies House registration for a UK registered office, the delivery policy for a stated UK dispatch location and Royal Mail or UK courier service, and the returns address for a UK location. Any one of these alone can be misleading; all three consistent with each other is a reasonably strong confirmation.

### Why this matters specifically for retatrutide

Retatrutide bought from outside the UK faces customs risk on import, since research chemicals and peptides can be delayed, inspected, or seized depending on how a parcel is declared crossing the border. A genuinely UK-based seller shipping domestically avoids this entirely, which is worth confirming rather than assuming from branding alone.

## Check Companies House for a UK registered office

Search the seller's stated company name on Companies House. A UK limited company has a registered office address within the UK, which is a legal requirement, not a marketing claim. If the company doesn't appear on Companies House at all, that's a strong indication it isn't a UK-registered entity, regardless of what the website's branding suggests.

### A UK company can still ship from abroad

It's worth noting that a UK-registered company can still choose to warehouse and dispatch stock from overseas. Companies House registration confirms the legal entity is UK-based, not necessarily where the physical product ships from, which is why the delivery policy check matters as a separate step.

## Check the delivery policy for a stated UK dispatch point

Look for a delivery policy stating UK dispatch specifically, using Royal Mail Tracked services or a named UK courier, with realistic delivery windows of 2-4 working days. A site quoting delivery windows of one to three weeks, or mentioning customs clearance for domestic UK orders, is very likely dispatching from overseas.

### Check the currency and pricing display too

While not conclusive on its own, pricing displayed only in a non-GBP currency, or a checkout that converts from another currency, is a secondary sign the operation isn't primarily UK-based, even if the site otherwise uses British spelling and UK-style branding.

## Check the returns address

If a returns policy is stated, check whether the address given for returns is UK-based. A UK company with an overseas returns address is an inconsistency worth asking about directly, since it suggests the actual fulfilment operation may not match the legal entity's location.

### Ask directly if anything is unclear

If any of these checks come back ambiguous, email and ask directly where orders are dispatched from. A genuinely UK-based seller answers this immediately and specifically; hesitation or a non-answer is itself informative.

## What UK-based looks like done properly

A seller such as Enzo Meds shows a consistent picture across all three checks: a UK Companies House entry, UK dispatch using Royal Mail Tracked services, and UK-based contact details, which is the pattern worth expecting from any seller claiming to be UK-based.

## Check the language and spelling conventions

UK-based sellers typically use British spelling consistently (colour, organised, litre) throughout their site, including in policy pages that are easier to overlook than the homepage. A site that's mostly British in tone but slips into American spelling or phrasing in its terms or delivery policy can be a sign that content, or the business itself, originates elsewhere.

### Phone numbers and area codes

If a phone number is provided, a genuine UK landline number will have a recognisable UK area code. A stated UK address paired with a phone number that clearly isn't a UK number is a specific, checkable inconsistency worth asking the seller about directly before you order.

## In short

Check Companies House for a UK registered office, the delivery policy for UK dispatch and realistic delivery windows, and the returns address for UK consistency. All three lining up is a reliable confirmation; any one contradicting the others is worth investigating before you order.
