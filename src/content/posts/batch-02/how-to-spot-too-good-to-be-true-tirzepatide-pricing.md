## Know the realistic range first

As of 2026, established UK sellers commonly price tirzepatide around £70-£110 for a 5mg vial and £120-£180 for a 10mg vial. A price meaningfully below that range, particularly on a new or thinly documented site, is one of the clearer signs something's been cut, usually the testing, the actual dosage, or both, rather than a genuine deal.

### Why tirzepatide pricing sits where it does

Genuine batch-specific testing (HPLC-UV through a real lab) has a real cost per batch, and sellers who do this properly build that cost into the price. Combine that with the fact that tirzepatide is heavily searched, and there's constant pressure from low-quality sellers trying to undercut on price precisely because it's the easiest way to win a search-driven sale.

## What unrealistically low pricing usually means

Underpriced tirzepatide is disproportionately likely to be underdosed, mislabelled, or old stock sold without fresh testing. A price alone doesn't prove any of this, but it's a reasonable trigger to look harder at the COA and company registration before ordering rather than treating the price itself as the main selling point.

### Aggressive discount codes and countdown timers

A "70% off, ends tonight" banner has nothing to do with how a peptide was actually sourced or tested. It's a pressure tactic borrowed from general ecommerce, and it's worth treating a peptide listing using this kind of urgency messaging with more scrutiny than a plain, steadily priced listing from an established seller.

## Compare price per mg, not the headline number

Check the exact vial strength before comparing prices across sellers. A "cheap" price that turns out to be for a 2.5mg vial isn't actually cheaper than another seller's 5mg vial once you adjust for strength. Always normalise to price per mg before deciding a listing is genuinely underpriced.

### Legitimate bulk pricing versus unrealistic discounts

A modest per-unit saving on a multi-vial order is normal and doesn't undermine legitimacy. A bulk discount so steep that even the single-unit price sits well under the realistic range is a different signal entirely and worth the same scrutiny as any other too-low price.

## Check the documentation before dismissing a low price

Before writing off a lower-priced listing, check whether it has a batch-specific COA, a findable Companies House entry, and reasonable delivery terms. A genuinely leaner operation with real documentation can price somewhat below the market average. A listing with none of these backing up a low price is telling you the price is the least surprising problem with it.

## Check whether the price has changed recently

If you've looked at a listing before, note whether the price has dropped sharply since your last visit with no clear explanation, a sale, a stated reason. A sudden, unexplained price drop on a specialised product like this is worth the same scrutiny as an unusually low price from a seller you're checking for the first time.

## What genuine testing costs a seller

Independent HPLC-UV testing through an accredited lab carries a real per-batch cost, on top of reference standards and staff time to prepare and submit samples. A seller genuinely testing every batch has a cost floor built into their pricing that a low-quality operation skipping this step doesn't have, which is a major reason the price gap between well-documented and poorly documented tirzepatide can be so wide.

### Look at the full checkout price, not just the listing

Some sellers bundle tracked delivery and proper cold-chain packaging into their headline price, while others add these as extras at checkout. Compare the final total, not just the advertised unit price, before deciding whether a listing is genuinely cheaper than another or simply structured differently.

## In short

Know the typical UK range, roughly £70-£110 for 5mg and £120-£180 for 10mg as of 2026, and treat pricing well below that, especially paired with aggressive discount messaging, as a reason to check the COA and company registration more closely rather than a reason to buy quickly.
