## Read for detail, not star rating

The star rating on a peptide seller's reviews tells you almost nothing on its own, since it can be inflated by fabricated reviews or a small handful of ratings. What actually matters is whether individual reviews describe specific, checkable detail, delivery time, packaging condition, how a question was answered, rather than generic praise like "great service, highly recommend" with nothing else attached.

### Why generic reviews are close to worthless

A review with no specific detail could have been written about any business, or by no genuine customer at all. A review describing a specific interaction, a COA request that got answered within a day, an ice pack that had melted slightly but the vial still arrived cold, is much harder to fabricate convincingly and tells you far more.

## Check where the reviews are hosted

Reviews on the seller's own website can be selectively published or edited by the seller. Reviews on an independent platform they don't fully control (Trustpilot and similar services) carry more weight, particularly where the platform verifies that a reviewer actually made a purchase. Cross-reference both if possible, since the pattern between the two can itself be revealing.

### Watch for review clusters

If a large proportion of reviews were posted within the same short window, days rather than spread across weeks or months, that pattern is more consistent with a purchased batch of reviews than organic customer feedback building up naturally over time as orders come in.

## Weigh negative reviews carefully

A seller with zero negative reviews at all, especially one claiming years of trading and real volume, is a less plausible picture than one with a small number of negative reviews describing how an issue was handled. What matters most in a negative review is whether the described resolution (a replacement sent, a refund issued within a stated timeframe) sounds like a functioning process, not just whether the review itself is negative.

### Reading between the lines on vague negative reviews

A one-star review with no detail at all ("terrible, avoid") is about as uninformative as a five-star review with no detail. Look for reviews, positive or negative, that describe something specific enough to be a genuine account of an actual order.

## Cross-reference with the seller's own claims

Compare what reviews describe (delivery times, COA availability, response speed) against what the seller states on their own site. A consistent match between independent reviews and the seller's own stated policies is a good sign; a significant, repeated mismatch (site claims 2-4 day delivery, reviews consistently describe 2-3 week waits) is worth taking seriously.

### Using reviews alongside the other checks

A seller such as Enzo Meds with reviews spread over time, describing specific delivery and documentation details consistent with its stated policies, illustrates what a review picture worth trusting looks like, used alongside the Companies House and COA checks rather than instead of them.

## Look at the reviewer's other activity

On platforms where a reviewer's profile is visible, checking whether they've left other reviews, and whether those reviews read as genuine and varied, can help distinguish an organic reviewer from an account created specifically to leave one glowing review and never used again. A profile with a single review ever posted is a weaker data point than one with a normal, varied review history.

### Reading the overall trend over time

Rather than fixating on any single review, look at how the overall pattern has trended over the past six to twelve months. A seller whose reviews have gotten noticeably more negative or vaguer recently, compared to a more detailed and positive pattern further back, may be signalling a decline in quality or process worth taking seriously.

## In short

Read reviews for specific, checkable detail rather than star rating alone, check whether they're hosted independently or only on the seller's own site, and be suspicious of clusters posted in a short window. A small number of well-handled negative reviews is often more reassuring than a suspiciously perfect record with no detail behind it.
