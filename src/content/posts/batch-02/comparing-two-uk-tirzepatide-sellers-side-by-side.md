## Compare across five categories

When choosing between two UK tirzepatide sellers, compare them directly across company registration, COA quality, pricing, delivery terms, and customer service response, rather than judging each in isolation. Because tirzepatide is so widely searched, there are usually several similar-looking listings to choose from, and a direct comparison is the fastest way to see which one is actually stronger.

### Don't start with price

It's tempting to compare price first, but price only matters once you know both sellers are offering genuinely comparable products. A listing with a batch-specific COA and a verifiable company behind it isn't the same product as a similarly priced listing with neither, even if both say "10mg tirzepatide."

## Compare company registration

Look up both sellers' stated company names on Companies House. Compare the incorporation date, filing status, and whether accounts or a confirmation statement have actually been filed for each. A company trading for two or more years with consistent filings is generally a stronger position, though a newer company can still be reasonable if the other checks hold up well.

### Check registered addresses

Note whether each registered address looks like a genuine, specific business location or a shared formation-agent address used by many unrelated companies. On its own this proves little, but weighed alongside the other checks it can tip a close comparison one way.

## Compare the COA on each listing

Check the batch number, test date, testing method, and purity figure shown on each seller's current tirzepatide COA. A specific, recent batch number (for example TZ-250601-11) with a named lab and method reflects a more serious testing process than a generic or outdated document. If one seller's COA is missing entirely and the other shows one, that's usually decisive on its own.

### Vial photos and batch matching

Where both sellers show real product photos, check whether the visible batch number matches the COA for each. A mismatch on either side is a stronger reason to avoid that seller than a small price gap in the other seller's favour.

## Compare pricing and delivery terms

Tirzepatide commonly runs £120-£180 for a 10mg vial from established UK sellers as of 2026. If one seller sits within that range and the other is well below it, treat the lower price as a reason to scrutinise their documentation more closely rather than as a simple advantage. Compare stated dispatch windows and delivery services too; tracked delivery within 2-4 working days with insulated packaging is the standard worth expecting from both.

### Compare returns policies directly

Note whether each seller states a clear returns process, including a reporting window and what evidence is needed. A seller with a specific written policy is offering more protection than one with only a vague "get in touch" line.

## Test both with the same question

Email both sellers an identical, specific pre-order question, such as asking for the batch-matching COA, and compare how each responds in speed and substance. This test alone often clarifies which seller is worth ordering from more than any other single check.

### A reference point for the comparison

Running both sellers through the same checks used against a listing like Enzo Meds, which states its company details, batch-numbered COA, and delivery terms clearly, gives you a concrete third point of comparison rather than weighing two unknowns against each other.

## Write it down as a simple comparison

Putting both sellers' details side by side in a simple table, company status, COA specificity, price per mg, delivery window, response time, makes a genuine difference easier to spot than trying to compare two open browser tabs from memory. This is particularly useful when both sites look similarly professional at first glance.

### Weigh categories together, not in isolation

A seller with a slightly better price but a thinner COA isn't automatically the better choice over one with a marginally higher price and a more specific, verifiable testing document. Consider all five categories together rather than letting price alone decide, especially for a first order with a seller you haven't used before.

## In short

Compare company registration, COA specificity, pricing against the market range, delivery and returns terms, and customer service response. Choose the seller stronger across most categories, not necessarily the cheapest one.
