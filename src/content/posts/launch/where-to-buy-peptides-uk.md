## "Where" is really "which criteria"

There's no single correct answer to where to buy peptides in the UK; there are, however, consistent criteria worth applying to any seller you're considering. Framing the question this way, criteria first, specific seller second, leads to a more reliable decision than starting from a list of names.

### The criteria that matter most

Batch-specific COA availability, UK stock (rather than shipping from overseas per order), clear cold-chain shipping practice, and a way to ask questions before ordering. These four cover most of what separates a considered purchase from a risky one.

## Domestic UK stock versus overseas shipping

A supplier holding genuine UK stock avoids customs delays and reduces the total transit time a temperature-sensitive product spends outside controlled conditions. This is worth confirming directly rather than assuming from a ".co.uk" domain or "UK-based" wording alone, since a UK-registered business can still ship from overseas stock per order.

### Reading between the lines of a listing

A listing heavy on marketing language but light on specifics, batch numbers, testing methods, shipping detail, is worth treating with more scrutiny than one that volunteers those specifics upfront.

## Building your own shortlist

Rather than looking for a single "correct" answer, apply the criteria above to a few candidates and compare how each one actually answers, not just what each one claims.
