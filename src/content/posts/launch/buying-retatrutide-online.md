## Why retatrutide specifically deserves extra care

As a newer peptide compared with more established GLP-1 drugs, retatrutide has a smaller base of community experience and independent testing data to draw on. That makes the specific seller's own documentation even more important, since there's less external verification to lean on.

### What a retatrutide-specific COA should show

The COA should confirm the compound is actually retatrutide (not a different or similarly named peptide), its measured purity, and the batch number matching the vial received.

## Checking the mg content carefully

Retatrutide vials are sold in a range of sizes; confirming the exact mg content against the COA, rather than assuming from the listing title alone, avoids a mismatch that would throw off every subsequent dosing calculation.

### Questions worth asking before ordering

Whether the batch has a COA available specifically confirming it as retatrutide, what testing method was used, and how the order will be shipped given the cold-chain sensitivity of the product.

## Starting cautiously

For a first retatrutide order specifically, starting with a smaller quantity from a seller who's cleared the documentation and shipping checks is a reasonable way to verify quality directly before committing to a larger order.
