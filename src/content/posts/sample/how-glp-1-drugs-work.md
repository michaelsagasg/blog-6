## What GLP-1 actually is

GLP-1, short for glucagon-like peptide-1, is a hormone your gut releases after you eat. It tells your pancreas to release insulin, slows how fast food leaves your stomach, and signals your brain that you're full. Drugs in this class copy that hormone's effect, lasting far longer than the natural version, which breaks down within minutes.

Retatrutide and tirzepatide go further. Tirzepatide also activates the GIP receptor, and retatrutide activates GIP and glucagon receptors on top of GLP-1.

### Why purity and sourcing matter here

Because these are complex peptide molecules, not simple small-molecule tablets, how they're manufactured, tested and shipped has a real effect on what actually ends up in the vial. A drug that works well in trials only works the same way for you if what you receive matches what was tested.

## Why this makes the seller part of the equation

Unlike buying a well-regulated pharmaceutical from a licensed pharmacy, this market has a wide range of sellers with varying levels of rigour. The chemistry of how the drug works doesn't change, but whether a specific vial actually contains what it claims to depends entirely on the seller behind it.

### What "legitimate" means in practice

A legitimate seller in this space is one that can back up its product with batch-specific lab testing, not just a confident-sounding product description.

## The takeaway

Understanding how these drugs work is only half the picture. Knowing that the seller matters just as much as the molecule is what the rest of this site is about.
